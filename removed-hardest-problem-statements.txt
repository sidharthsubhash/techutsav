CATALYST 2030 - REMOVED HARDEST PROBLEM STATEMENTS

These are the five most technically demanding challenges selected from each track, ranked by implementation breadth, AI complexity, and integration requirements.

==================== 2ND YEAR ====================

1. PS-2Y-13 - Circular Economy Tracker
SDG 12 - Responsible Consumption

Challenge:
Design a smart circular economy tracker and reverse logistics network for consumer electronics to extend product lifespans and eliminate electronic waste (e-waste).

AI component:
Use AI to monitor device lifecycles, identify repair or reuse opportunities, and predict the best route for repair, refurbishment, recycling, or material recovery.

Full-stack component:
Build a lifecycle dashboard and reverse logistics network that tracks electronic products across their lifecycle and maps collection, repair, resale, and recycling workflows.

Key deliverables:
- Lifecycle tracking for consumer electronics and components.
- AI recommendations for repair, resale, remanufacturing, or recycling.
- Reverse logistics workflow and collection network model.
- Dashboard showing e-waste reduction, recovery, and product lifespan metrics.


2. PS-2Y-15 - Eco-Report
SDG 14/15 - Life Below Water / Life on Land

Challenge:
Create a platform where users upload an image of an environmental issue such as litter, damaged vegetation, or polluted water. AI categorizes the issue and displays it on a community map.

AI component:
Use image or text classification to categorize uploaded environmental issue images as litter, vegetation damage, water pollution, or similar categories.

Full-stack component:
Build an image upload interface, a community map displaying reported issues by location, and a reports view showing categorized issues and trends.

Key deliverables:
- Environmental issue image upload with location tagging.
- AI-powered issue categorization.
- Interactive community map with issue markers.
- Reports dashboard with category trends and statistics.


3. PS-2Y-11 - Campus Problem-Solving Portal
SDG 9 - Industry & Innovation

Challenge:
Create a platform where students submit real-world campus or community problems. AI automatically categorizes problems and groups similar submissions to identify recurring issues.

AI component:
Use text classification and clustering to categorize submissions and cluster similar problems.

Full-stack component:
Build a student submission form and an admin dashboard showing categories, issue clusters, trends, and recurring concerns.

Key deliverables:
- Problem submission interface.
- Automatic categorization and clustering.
- Admin dashboard with issue trends and clusters.
- Identification of recurring issues.


4. PS-2Y-12 - Smart Parking Finder
SDG 11 - Sustainable Cities & Communities

Challenge:
Develop a web application that displays available parking spaces from user-provided or simulated data, predicts peak parking periods, and suggests alternatives.

AI component:
Predict peak parking occupancy from historical or simulated patterns and suggest optimal alternative parking locations.

Full-stack component:
Build an interactive map or grid of parking spaces with availability, peak-time predictions, alternative suggestions, and historical data.

Key deliverables:
- Real-time parking availability display.
- Peak-period prediction.
- Alternative parking suggestions.
- Data persistence and historical trend analysis.


5. PS-2Y-10 - Resume Skill Analyzer
SDG 8 - Decent Work

Challenge:
Build a web application that analyzes a resume against a selected job description to identify matching skills, missing skills, and areas for improvement.

AI component:
Use natural language processing to extract skills from resumes, compare them with job requirements, and identify gaps.

Full-stack component:
Build resume upload and parsing, job-description selection or input, and a visual skill-match dashboard with improvement suggestions.

Key deliverables:
- Resume upload and parsing.
- Job description selection or input.
- NLP skill extraction and matching.
- Visual analysis dashboard with improvement suggestions.


==================== 3RD YEAR ====================

1. PS-3Y-12 - Digital Product Passport & Reverse Logistics Engine
SDG 12 - Responsible Consumption

Challenge:
Design a trustless, automated Digital Product Passport framework combined with a computer-vision-driven reverse logistics engine to maximize component recovery, remanufacturing, and cross-industry material circularity for consumer electronics.

AI component:
Use computer vision to identify device condition, component usability, and material composition, and connect those findings to a secure product passport for lifecycle decisions.

Full-stack component:
Build a passport platform and reverse logistics dashboard that records provenance, condition, and material data, then routes devices through repair, remanufacturing, or recycling.

Key deliverables:
- Tamper-resistant product passport.
- Computer-vision assessment of device condition and components.
- Component-level recovery and remanufacturing decision engine.
- Material circularity and e-waste reduction analytics dashboard.


2. PS-3Y-09 - Predictive Maintenance Dashboard
SDG 9 - Industry & Innovation

Challenge:
Using machine-sensor data, predict whether equipment is likely to fail based on temperature, vibration, and operating conditions.

AI component:
Build a classification or anomaly-detection model to predict imminent equipment failures and flag machines requiring preventive maintenance.

Full-stack component:
Build a real-time monitoring dashboard showing sensor readings, health scores, failure-risk indicators, and maintenance alerts.

Key deliverables:
- Sensor-data processing and feature engineering.
- Equipment-failure prediction model.
- Real-time monitoring dashboard with health scores.
- Alert system for maintenance scheduling.


3. PS-3Y-15 - Biodiversity Intelligence Platform
SDG 15 - Life on Land

Challenge:
Build a web application where users upload plant or animal images. The system identifies the species using an AI model or API and maintains a biodiversity database with observation and distribution data.

AI component:
Use computer vision to identify species from photographs and return confidence-scored identifications.

Full-stack component:
Build image upload, species-result display, an observation database, and a distribution map showing where species have been seen.

Key deliverables:
- Image upload and species identification.
- Confidence-scored AI recognition.
- Biodiversity observation database.
- Species distribution map and statistics.


4. PS-3Y-10 - Innovation Idea Intelligence Platform
SDG 9 - Industry & Innovation

Challenge:
Create a platform where users submit technological ideas. AI categorizes ideas, detects similar submissions, identifies problem domains, and generates a basic feasibility and impact summary.

AI component:
Use NLP and clustering to categorize ideas, detect semantic duplicates, identify trends, and generate feasibility or impact assessments.

Full-stack component:
Build an idea submission portal and admin dashboard showing categories, similarity clusters, trending domains, and feasibility summaries.

Key deliverables:
- Idea submission and categorization portal.
- Similarity and duplicate detection.
- Problem-domain identification and trend analysis.
- Automated feasibility and impact summaries.


5. PS-3Y-11 - Traffic Congestion Predictor
SDG 11 - Sustainable Cities & Communities

Challenge:
Use a historical traffic dataset to predict congestion levels for selected roads and time periods, visualize hotspots, and suggest alternative routes.

AI component:
Build a prediction model that forecasts congestion levels for roads and time windows and identifies recurring hotspot patterns.

Full-stack component:
Build a dashboard with a color-coded congestion map, time-based prediction selectors, and alternative route suggestions.

Key deliverables:
- Historical traffic analysis and model training.
- Congestion prediction by road and time period.
- Interactive congestion-hotspot map.
- Alternative route recommendations.
